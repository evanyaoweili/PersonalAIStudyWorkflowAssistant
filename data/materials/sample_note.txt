CMU AI Agent Course - Sample Study Note

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combines a retriever, which fetches
relevant chunks of text from a knowledge base, with a generator (an LLM)
that uses those chunks as context to answer a question. This grounds the
model's answer in real source material and reduces hallucination.

The typical RAG pipeline has three stages:
1. Ingestion - split documents into chunks and embed them into a vector store.
2. Retrieval - given a query, embed it and find the most similar chunks.
3. Generation - pass the retrieved chunks plus the query to an LLM to
   produce a grounded answer.
