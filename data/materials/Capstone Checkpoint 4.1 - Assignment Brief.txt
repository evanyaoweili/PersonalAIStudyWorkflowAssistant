Learning outcome addressed
3. Integrate Tree-of-Thought reasoning using CrewAI, LangChain, and MCP into the capstone
   agent design.

In this module, you moved from linear reasoning to structured exploration. You learned how
ToT reasoning improves accuracy by generating multiple candidate reasoning paths, evaluating
intermediate states, and pruning weak branches. You also began mapping ToT roles to
implementation tools, including CrewAI, LangChain, and MCP.

This checkpoint focuses on design considerations rather than full implementation. You will
update your capstone proposal to include a ToT reasoning approach or an alternative
reasoning approach, identify the agent roles required to support structured reasoning, and
map those roles to tools you will use in later modules. The goal is to make your reasoning
architecture explicit and testable.

Directions

In a short written submission, complete Item 1. Then complete either Item 2 or Item 3,
depending on whether your project uses Tree of Thought (ToT) reasoning.

1. Decide whether ToT reasoning is appropriate for your agent. (Required)
   a. Explain whether ToT reasoning would improve your capstone design or hinder it.

2. If ToT is appropriate for your project, complete the following sections:
   1. Identify where ToT reasoning will be used within your capstone architecture.
      a. Describe the part of your workflow where ToT reasoning will be used.
      b. Explain why a linear chain of thought may fail in this area due to premature
         commitment, high branching, or constraint complexity.
   2. Define your ToT structure.
      a. Specify how reasoning will be represented as a tree: what a "thought" represents,
         what constitutes a node/branch/depth, the expected branching factor and depth
         limit, and when the system terminates and selects a final output.
   3. Describe your search and evaluation strategy.
      a. Choose one primary search strategy (BFS, DFS, beam search, or Monte Carlo-style
         sampling) and explain why it fits your use case.
      b. Explain how candidate branches will be assessed: evaluation criteria/rubric, who
         performs the evaluation, pruning thresholds and failure conditions, how ties are
         resolved, and how the strategy is constrained by compute, latency, or cost.
   4. Map ToT roles to implementation tools (at minimum: thought generator, critic/evaluator,
      decision maker/controller, memory/state manager), identifying which of CrewAI,
      LangChain, or MCP supports each role and why.
   5. Identify one risk (e.g. branch explosion, weak evaluation signals, overexploitation,
      inconsistent state management) and one mitigation strategy.

3. If ToT is not appropriate for your project, complete the following sections:
   1. Explain why ToT is not appropriate for your project as an intentional design decision.
   2. Identify the reasoning approach that best fits your project and briefly explain why it
      supports your agent's goal more effectively than ToT.

Submission instructions
Suggested word count: 750-1100 words.
