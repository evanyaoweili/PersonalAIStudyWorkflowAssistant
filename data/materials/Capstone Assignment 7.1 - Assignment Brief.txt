Required Capstone Assignment 7.1: Final Capstone Project Report and Presentation - Section C
Due: Monday by 9:29am | Points: 1 | Available after Aug 28 at 6am
Submitting: a file upload

Learning outcomes addressed
1. Integrate agent components developed throughout the program into a cohesive autonomous
   system.
2. Present an integrated autonomous agent system and its evaluation results to a technical
   audience.

This final capstone assignment is your opportunity to bring together the work you have
developed throughout the program into a complete, cohesive autonomous system. Across
Modules 1 through 6, you defined the problem, refined the agent design, incorporated
reasoning and memory, added retrieval and coordination strategies, and introduced
guardrails, evaluation, and human oversight. In this final assignment, you will synthesize
those decisions into a polished report and a recorded presentation for a technical audience.

You will submit two required deliverables:
1. A final capstone report using the template provided in Self-Study Capstone Checkpoint
   Assignment 7.1: Final Capstone Planning.
2. A presentation using the software of your choice, and a recorded 8-10 minute presentation
   video to accompany the report.
3. You may also choose to submit an optional 90-second elevator pitch recording (not graded,
   but used by Learning Facilitators to select a showcase for Professor Amir Farimani).

Directions

1. Final capstone report
Your final report should present a clear, well-organized account of your project, including:
- The problem your system addresses and the intended user
- The goal and scope of the system
- The final architecture and major components
- How the design evolved across the program
- An overview of the implementation
- Evaluation methods and results
- Safety, reliability, and human oversight considerations
- Current limitations and future improvements
- A link to your public GitHub repository

Your public GitHub repository should be included in the report and enable a technical
audience to understand and review your project. At a minimum, the repository should include:
- A README that explains the project, architecture, setup, and usage
- The main code or notebooks
- Any supporting materials you want to share, such as sample inputs, outputs, or evaluation
  artifacts
- Clear instructions for running or reviewing the project

2. Final presentation and recorded video
Create a presentation and record an 8-10 minute presentation video, designed for a technical
audience, clearly explaining:
- The problem and why it matters
- The goal of your autonomous system
- The architecture and major components
- The key design decisions made across the program
- The evaluation approach and the most important results
- The public GitHub repository and what it contains
- The main strengths, limitations, and next steps for the system

3. Record and submit your video
Record your required 8-10 minute video and host it on an accessible platform (YouTube,
Vimeo, or Loom), ideally unlisted/private-share so your instructor and Learning Facilitator
can view it. Submit the video link in a document that also includes a two-to-three-sentence
summary of your presentation, uploaded to Canvas together with your final report.

Optional deliverable: a 90-second elevator pitch recording, clearly labeled as optional,
covering the problem, the agentic AI system designed, the value of the solution, and the
most important takeaway.

Submission instructions
Suggested word count for the final report: 1000-1500 words.
Suggested presentation length: 8-10 slides for a 10-minute presentation.
